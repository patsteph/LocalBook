About

LocalBook is a private research notebook that runs on your Mac. Add documents, web pages, notes and meeting recordings, then search them and ask questions. The AI runs on your own computer, so your material never goes to a cloud service.

The iPhone app pairs with your Mac over your own network. Your chosen notebooks sync to the phone, and searching and answering happen on the phone. It can record and transcribe meetings with named speakers, and make briefings, quizzes, flashcards, audio overviews and visuals.

Source and Mac downloads: github.com/patsteph/LocalBook. The iPhone app is in TestFlight beta by invitation and needs a Mac running LocalBook 2.5.5 or later.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-27

LocalBook collects no data. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

  • Your notebooks, questions, notes, recordings and generated items stay on your Mac and your phone. They sync only between your own devices, directly and encrypted, over your local network.
  • Questions are answered by AI models that run on your device. Nothing you write or say is sent to me or to any AI provider.
  • Speech is turned into text on the phone using Apple's on-device speech recognition.
  • On first use the app downloads AI model files from public model hosts. Nothing about you is sent with that download.
  • The app asks for the microphone (recording), speech recognition (transcripts), the local network (finding your Mac) and the camera (scanning the pairing code). You can turn each off in Settings.
  • If you send feedback through TestFlight or email, I receive what you choose to send.

Questions about this policy: stephensuc@gmail.com.

Support

Email stephensuc@gmail.com, or open an issue at github.com/patsteph/LocalBook/issues. If the phone cannot find your Mac, check that both are on the same network, that LocalBook is running on the Mac, and that Local Network access is allowed for the app in iOS Settings.
